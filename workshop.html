What you'll learn

The sleep science they don't put in blog posts.

Most sleep content covers the same ground. This workshop goes deeper: the actual research mechanisms, why common advice often doesn't work, and what the data says about what does.

01 /

Sleep architecture & why your deep sleep matters

What's actually happening in each sleep stage, why deep sleep is the one most people sabotage without knowing it, and what your Oura score is actually measuring.

02 /

Caffeine: the half-life problem

Why your 2pm coffee is still affecting your deep sleep at midnight. The research on caffeine metabolism, timing, and how to calculate your personal cut-off time.

03 /

Alcohol & the REM debt you don't know you're taking

A glass of wine at dinner feels like it helps you sleep. The data says otherwise. What alcohol actually does to your sleep stages — and when the effect wears off.

04 /

Jet lag: the protocol that actually works

Light exposure timing, melatonin dosing, strategic napping, and the order that matters. How to arrive functional instead of wrecked.

05 /

Gadgets worth buying (and the ones that aren't)

The sleep gadgets that show measurable effects in real data — sleep masks, white noise, temperature tools — and which ones are marketing theater.

06 /

Reading your own Oura data

What your readiness score, HRV baseline, and sleep stage distribution are actually telling you — and what questions to ask when scores drop unexpectedly.
